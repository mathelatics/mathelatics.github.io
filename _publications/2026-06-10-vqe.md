We present a Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) implementation in Qiskit targeting ground-state energies of small Hamiltonians — the Heisenberg spin chain and a minimal-basis H₂ molecule. A parameterized real-amplitudes ansatz is optimized with COBYLA/SLSQP to minimize the energy expectation value ⟨ψ(θ)|H|ψ(θ)⟩, with Pauli terms measured shot-by-shot. Results converge to the exact diagonalization ground-state energy within a few hundred iterations for two-qubit systems, and deeper ansätze capture correlation energy beyond mean-field. The work quantifies the shot-noise and ansatz-depth trade-offs that govern VQE accuracy on NISQ hardware.
