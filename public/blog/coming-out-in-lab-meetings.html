Coming Out in Lab Meetings (and Other Phase Transitions)

Coming out is a phase transition: order parameter, critical point, hysteresis. I got into physics partly because that metaphor felt safe. It wasn't. But the lab turned out to be where it mattered most.

What worked

  • Pronouns on my badge before any policy required it
    • Colleagues mirrored it within a semester
    • Students asked for theirs too
  • Mentoring pair-programming sessions for queer undergrads
  • Saying "when my partner…" out loud in ordinary contexts

What backfired

  1. Waiting for the department to act first
  2. Treating every misgendering as a tribunal case instead of a teaching moment
  3. Silence during Pride month (felt like cowardice; was read as coldness)

Visibility is a measurement you perform on your own behalf — collapse the wavefunction of assumption into fact.

A small protocol

  • ☑ Put pronouns somewhere low-stakes (badge, email sig, slides footer)
  • ☑ Mention your partner naturally, at least once per week
  • ☐ Wait until you feel "ready" (you never will; do it anyway)

If you're a student reading this at 2am: the field needs you exactly as coherent as you are.
