# About

I decode noise into knowledge.

I am a quantum information scientist working at the intersection of superconducting qubits, quantum error correction, and open scientific software. My group's mission: make fault-tolerant quantum computing real — and make the path into quantum science welcoming for everyone, especially LGBTQ+ and underrepresented folks who too often get measured out of the wavefunction.

As an openly queer, nonbinary-leaning (she/they) academic, I believe representation is a form of error correction for the scientific community: it catches the bit-flips of bias before they propagate through a career. I mentor queer STEM students, review for inclusive hiring, and build tools that lower the barrier to entry into quantum research.

# Research Areas

My research treats coherence as both a physical resource and a human one. Five active thrusts:

01

Fault-Tolerant Architectures

Surface & color codes, lattice surgery, and magic-state distillation with realistic noise models. Goal: reduce the overhead from logical qubit to useful circuit by 10×.

Surface CodesLattice SurgeryThreshold Theorems
02

Superconducting Qubit Hardware

Transmon design, 3D cavities, and parametric couplers. We chase T1/T2 beyond 500 µs while keeping fabrication yield above 90%.

TransmonsCircuit QEDCryogenics
03

Quantum Machine Learning

Barren-plateau avoidance, quantum kernels for materials discovery, and NISCA-era hybrid pipelines that actually beat classical baselines.

Variational AlgorithmsQuantum KernelsHybrid Pipelines
04

Open Quantum Software

Maintainer tooling for reproducible experiments: calibration daemons, schema'd pulse schedules, and simulation backends anyone can audit.

ReproducibilityPythonCUDA
05

Ethics, Policy & Inclusion in Quantum

Quantum technology governance, export-control equity, and workforce studies on LGBTQ+ retention in physics departments.

PolicyWorkforce StudiesEthics
