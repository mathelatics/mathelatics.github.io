## Overview

The Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) is a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm for approximating the ground-state energy of a Hamiltonian \(H\). It is one of the leading near-term quantum algorithms because it works with shallow circuits that fit within NISQ coherence limits, offloading the optimization to a classical computer.

## What I built

I implemented VQE in Qiskit with the following pieces:

- **Ansatz:** a two-qubit (and scalable n-qubit) parameterized circuit built from rotation gates and entangling CNOT layers (real-amplitudes style).
- **Hamiltonians:** Pauli-string decompositions of simple Hamiltonians, including the Heisenberg spin chain and a hydrogen-like \(H_2\) minimal-basis Hamiltonian.
- **Classical optimizer:** COBYLA and SLSQP-driven parameter updates that minimize \(\langle \psi(\theta) | H | \psi(\theta) \rangle\).
- **Measurement:** expectation values estimated by measuring each Pauli term in its eigenbasis and averaging over shots.

## Results

For the two-qubit Heisenberg and \(H_2\) Hamiltonians, VQE converges to the exact ground-state energy (verified by classical diagonalization) within a few hundred optimization iterations. Energy estimates match the analytic result to within shot noise, and increasing the ansatz depth recovers correlation energy that a mean-field approximation misses.
