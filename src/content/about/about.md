## Who I am

I'm **Dr. Alex Rivera** (she/they), a quantum scientist working at the intersection of *superconducting qubits*, *quantum error correction*, and open science. My research asks a simple question with complicated answers: **how do fragile quantum states survive a noisy world?**

The answer, so far, has three parts: better codes, better devices, and better communities. All three are load-bearing.

> "Representation is error correction for the scientific community — it protects the logical information (talent) from the physical noise (exclusion)."

## The path so far

- PhD in physics, then postdoc building superconducting processors
- Fell into queer academia, stayed to make the door wider behind me
- Now an assistant professor running a small, loud, proud lab

Focus tags: `quantum computing` · `error correction` · `open hardware` · inclusive STEM

## What I care about

1. **Rigor with warmth.** Peer review can be kind without being soft.
2. **Reproducibility.** If your experiment can't be rebuilt from your paper, it isn't finished.
3. **Access.** Paywalls, gatekeeping, and unlisted pronouns are all bugs, not features.

## Teaching & mentoring (summary)

I've taught graduate and introductory quantum courses, mentored 14 PhD students, 9 postdocs, and 30+ undergrads — with a deliberate focus on women, gender-minority, and LGBTQ+ scholars. Full course list, mentoring record, awards, and service live on my **[Curriculum Vitae page →](cv.html)**.
