title: Suppressing ZZ Crosstalk in Fixed-Frequency Transmon Arrays via Flux-Shunted Couplers

Static ZZ interactions remain a dominant error source in fixed-frequency transmon lattices. We introduce a flux-shunted coupler architecture that reduces residual ZZ by two orders of magnitude while preserving fast two-qubit gates, verified across a 60-qubit processor.
